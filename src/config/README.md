# config

Edw kratame tis statheres, ta presets kai tis rythmiseis. Oi ypologismoi sta alla folders diavazoun aftes tis times.

## Arxeia

- [binaural.ts](binaural.ts): Orizw ta binaural options, ta direction-bin sets kai tis times gia HRTF EQ, interpolation kai coherence. Exw kai elegxous twn rythmiseon.
- [bras-cr2.ts](bras-cr2.ts): Krataw diastaseis, synthikes, source/receiver poses kai file paths gia to BRAS CR2 benchmark. Metatrepw kai tis syntetagmenes sto systima tou app.
- [directivity.ts](directivity.ts): Exw ta patterns tis pigis kai tis per-band parametrous tous. Krataw episis defaults, orientation limits kai tin emfanisi tou velous.
- [fdn-coloration.ts](fdn-coloration.ts): Orizw ta oria gia tin aksiologisi tou late FDN, opws echo density, spectral coloration kai periodicity. Den einai control tou kanonikou playback.
- [late-field.ts](late-field.ts): Krataw ta delay-line layouts tou late FDN. Kathe profile orizei poses grammes kathysterisis exw kai pos moirazontai oi xronoi tous.
- [materials-library.ts](materials-library.ts): Exw absorption kai scattering ana banda gia ta ylika. Krataw tis piges kai tis proseggiseis: oi times einai antiprosopeftikes, oxi metriseis tou sygkekrimenou dwmatiou.
- [probes.ts](probes.ts): Orizw probe spacing, clearances, bake budget, direction bins kai blending. Edw einai kai ta modes live, probes kai hybrid.
- [quality-presets.ts](quality-presets.ts): Krataw trace budgets kai scheduler thresholds. To app xrisimopoiei settled-full me to epilegmeno ray count, enw alla presets menoun gia baking kai sygkriseis.
- [ray-termination.ts](ray-termination.ts): Orizw pote stamataei ena ray apo energeia, xrono i bounce count. Metatrepw kai ta auto oria se times kai ypologizw to histogram size.
- [render-modes.ts](render-modes.ts): Orizw tis hybrid kai full-IR arxitektonikes, ta IR limits kai ta settings tou synthetic tail. Default tou app einai to hybrid.
- [room-presets.ts](room-presets.ts): Krataw ta dwmatia me diastaseis, surface mixes kai source/listener positions. Ypologizw ta meigmata ylikwn kai elegxw an ta presets einai egkyra.
- [specular-rays.ts](specular-rays.ts): Exw defaults kai oria gia ta echoes apo specular ray paths: amplitude mode, hits, order, energy conservation kai path memory.
