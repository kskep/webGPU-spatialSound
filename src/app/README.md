# app

Edw syndew ta diafora kommati tis efarmogis. Oi controllers kratane tin katastasi kai kaloun tous ypologismous, to tracing kai to audio.

## Arxeia

- [probe-controller.ts](probe-controller.ts): Diaxeirizomai to probe set, tis epiloges tou xristi, to baking kai to blending. Syndew ta probe modules me ti skini kai ton scheduler.
- [scene-editor.ts](scene-editor.ts): Krataw ti skini kai pernaw se afti tis allages apo ta controls. Fortwnw presets kai furniture scenes, kanw validation kai reset.
- [stage-timings.ts](stage-timings.ts): Krataw xronous gia ta stadia kathe update, opws GPU, acoustic update kai audio. Ypologizw statistika gia debug kai export.
- [trace-pipeline.ts](trace-pipeline.ts): Trexw ena request me ti swsti skini kai budget. Syndyazw mesh trace i probe data me early reflections kai echoes, kai enimerwnw apotelesma kai audio.
